## İnceleme notu (App Review Information > Notes)

```
No account or sign-in is needed.

AI: the assistant runs on the iPad. Tap Download in the assistant; it shows the size (2–6 GB depending on the iPad) and asks before downloading. Until the model is downloaded, the assistant answers with Apple's on-device model.

Purchases: the free plan has 15 AI answers a day, 1 summary and 3 study sets a week. To test Pro, use the yearly plan's 7-day free trial in the sandbox. Restore Purchases is on the Pro page and in Settings.

Background audio: start a lecture recording in a notebook and go to the Home Screen; the recording continues. Play an audio summary in Studio and lock the screen; it keeps playing.

Optional, not needed for review: in Settings the user can connect to an AI running on their own computer (Ollama, LM Studio) on the same Wi-Fi.
```

"Sign-in required" kapalı. İletişim bilgileri (ad, soyad, telefon, e-posta) kullanıcının kendisi tarafından girilecek.
