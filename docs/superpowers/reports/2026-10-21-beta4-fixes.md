# Mürekkep 3.0 beta 4 (build 16): text pen, assistant, video overview

Branch `murekkep-3-fix-textpen-ai`, after the user's report on build 15. These are the fixes and the calls made along the way.

## Text pen ("Neler oluyor?" and "için" read as garbage on a turned page)

Root causes, reproduced with the Python twins:

1. The writing was never levelled. On a page turned by −19° or −21°, the ink was that far off the page lines. The model already failed a few degrees off level, and the line splitter failed with it.
2. The line band's slope was fitted from the first three letters, so a capital followed by a descender split one line in two ("Bug" | "ün hava…").

Fixes:

- **Levelling:** `InkDeskew` levels the batch by searching for the angle with the sharpest projection profile. `readWithModel` reads the levelled ink, and so does Vision's raster. The boxes are mapped back to the page from where the writing starts.
- **Line band:** the slope is the Theil–Sen median of the last 12 letters, at most 12°, and is only fitted once the line has 6 letters. Before that the band is level.
  - Over 13 held-out fonts, 7 angles and 7 phrases, one written line was split 15 times in 1274 attempts; now it is split once.
  - Two stacked lines still come out as two, 130 of 130.
- **Model:** retrained with training lines turned up to ±8°.
  - Held-out CER is 3.42 %, down from 5.9 % at the start of the run.
  - On the user's three phrases, written in 13 held-out fonts at 7 angles, 253 of 273 are read exactly; the build 15 model read 244.
- **Training crash:** the run stopped because "β".upper() is the Greek capital "Β", which no font draws. An all-caps line that would leave the charset now stays as it is.

## Assistant (a scanned grammar page answered with "d", "s", "3")

This was done in its own session; its notes are in `textbook-exercises.md`.

- Scanned pages are read column by column, and printed blanks are kept as `___`.
- Textbook exercises are split into the instruction, the items, the gaps and the allowed words.
- On the iPad, the answer schema only allows those words. a/an follows the sound of the next word.

## Video overview (one part spoke English, the next Turkish)

Root cause:

- With "Otomatik", an English book could resolve to either language.
- The on-device model then wrote some scenes in the book's language.
- The offline writer put a frame in the chosen language around the book's own English sentences.
- All of it was voiced in one language.

Fixes:

- The automatic language is gone. A stored "auto" becomes the app's language.
- Scripts are told to translate the source.
- On-device scenes, plans and dialogue lines that come back in the other language are asked for once more. Anything still in the wrong language is left out.
- The create sheet opens page-sized. Language, length and level are on top. Sources that are not chosen are folded away, and every look is in view. When the sheet is at least 860 pt wide, it shows two columns.

## Rulings

- **Offline writer language.** The offline writer writes in the source's language when that differs from the chosen one, and says so while writing. It can only repeat the source's sentences, and mixing two languages is what the user reported. Cost if wrong: someone who chose Turkish gets an English offline video.
- **Wrong-language on-device scenes.** A scene still in the wrong language after one retry is dropped. Cost if wrong: a slightly shorter video.
- **Model choice.** The shipped model is the continued run (main4), chosen on held-out CER (3.42 % against 3.62 %). On the user's phrases it scored 253 against main3's 256, which is within noise. Cost if wrong: a few lines a little worse.
- **Six letters before a slope.** Lines shorter than six letters keep a level band. The batch is levelled first, so their slope matters little. Cost if wrong: a short line written at a steep angle to the rest of the batch may split.
